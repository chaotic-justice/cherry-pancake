# Product

## Register
product

## Users
One primary user managing a small-business bookkeeping workflow. She uses the app to turn recurring accounting exports into finished workbooks without command-line steps or filename conventions.

## Product Purpose
Provide a small, dependable toolbox for repetitive finance operations. Success means the user can supply source documents, understand what the tool needs, and download the result without technical help.

## Brand Personality
Calm, practical, friendly.

## Anti-references
Avoid enterprise dashboards, generic SaaS marketing patterns, decorative complexity, and interfaces that expose implementation details.

## Design Principles
- Make the next action obvious.
- Prefer familiar file workflows over instructions.
- Explain errors in terms of what the user can fix.
- Keep completed work easy to download and identify.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Support keyboard use, clear focus states, readable contrast, reduced motion, and status messaging that does not rely on color alone.
